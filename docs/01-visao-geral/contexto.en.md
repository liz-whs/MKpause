# Context

School life involves not only students' academic development but also situations of pressure, anxiety, frustration, conflict and overload that can affect their well-being and their participation in school activities.

In these situations, **self-regulation** strategies can help students recognize their emotional state and take actions that help them recover the conditions needed to continue their activities.

However, offering isolated resources is not enough. It is also important to have a structured way to record these situations and, when necessary, allow the responsible professionals to follow up.

**MKpause** emerges in this context as a platform that brings self-regulation resources and follow-up together.

Through the system, students can complete self-regulation sessions, record their emotional state before and after the activities, access content and view their history.

At the same time, professionals such as teachers, school psychologists and clinical psychologists have specific features to follow students according to their links and permissions.

In this way, MKpause seeks to build a bridge between **students' autonomy during self-regulation** and the **follow-up provided by the responsible professionals**.
