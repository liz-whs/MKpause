# Scope

The scope of MKpause covers features related to **student self-regulation**, the recording of completed sessions and the follow-up of this information by authorized users.

The platform has different access profiles, ensuring that each user can access only the features and information related to their role in the system.

---

## In Scope

### Student

The system must allow students to:

- sign in;
- access their personal area;
- start self-regulation sessions;
- record their mood before the session;
- access self-regulation games, videos and activities;
- record their mood after the session;
- record information about the experience;
- raise alerts in the situations defined by the system;
- view their session history.

### Teacher

The system must allow teachers to:

- sign in;
- view linked students;
- receive alerts about the students they follow;
- record notes about students.

### School Psychologist

The system must allow school psychologists to:

- sign in;
- view linked students;
- receive alerts;
- record notes;
- view the information needed for follow-up at school.

### Clinical Psychologist

The system must allow clinical psychologists to:

- sign in;
- access linked students;
- view the information made available for follow-up;
- receive alerts about the students they follow;
- access the reports and records permitted by the system.

### Administrator

The system must allow administrators to:

- sign in;
- register, view, update and remove students;
- manage teachers;
- manage psychologists;
- manage follow-up records;
- manage the links between students and professionals;
- maintain the records needed for the platform to operate.

### Guardian

The guardian takes part in the process through their link with the student.

Their access or link is not created at the same time as the student's initial registration. The intended process involves sending an invitation, or an equivalent mechanism, afterwards to establish the link.

---

## Out of Scope

MKpause is **not intended** to:

- provide psychological or psychiatric diagnosis;
- replace care provided by health professionals;
- prescribe medication or treatment;
- provide automated psychological care;
- act as an emergency service;
- make clinical decisions on its own;
- replace teachers, psychologists or any other professionals responsible for the student.

The data and resources provided by the system are intended to **support self-regulation and follow-up**, not to diagnose or treat.

---

## System Boundaries

MKpause acts as a support tool between the student and the professionals involved in their follow-up.

The system can record information, organize histories, provide activities and generate alerts according to predefined rules. However, the professional interpretation of the information and the decisions about the student's follow-up remain the responsibility of the qualified people involved in the process.

---

## Scope Evolution

The scope may be expanded during the project as new requirements are identified and validated.

New features must be documented before they are added to the system, keeping requirements, use cases, data modeling and implementation consistent.
