# Problem

Students may face moments of emotional overload during their school routine without a structured process to recognize their state, take a pause and use self-regulation strategies.

At the same time, teachers and the professionals responsible for follow-up may lack organized information about how often these situations occur, which strategies students use and the outcomes perceived after an intervention.

The absence of a centralized environment can make it harder to:

- give students quick access to self-regulation resources;
- record their emotional state before and after an activity;
- identify situations that require attention;
- record notes made by professionals;
- view a student's history;
- communicate situations that require follow-up;
- organize the information used by the different professionals involved.

## Central problem

> How can students be offered an accessible, structured way to carry out self-regulation activities in the school environment while also allowing the responsible professionals to record and follow up on these situations?

MKpause is proposed as a digital solution to support this process, bringing sessions, records, activities, alerts, notes and follow-up information together in one place.
