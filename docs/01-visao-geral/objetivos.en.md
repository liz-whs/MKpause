# Objectives

## General Objective

To develop a digital platform that helps students with **emotional self-regulation in the school environment**, offering resources for self-regulation sessions and allowing the responsible professionals to follow the information generated, according to their links and permissions.

---

## Specific Objectives

MKpause's specific objectives are to:

- provide students with an accessible environment for self-regulation sessions;
- allow students to record their emotional state before and after a session;
- offer activities, games, videos and other content that support self-regulation;
- record information about the sessions completed;
- keep a history of each student's sessions and records;
- allow students to view their own history;
- allow alerts to be raised in situations defined by the system's rules;
- forward alerts to the professionals responsible for follow-up;
- allow teachers and school psychologists to record notes about students;
- allow authorized professionals to view information relevant to follow-up;
- organize the links between students, teachers, psychologists and guardians;
- allow administrators to manage users, follow-up records and links;
- bring information about self-regulation and follow-up together on a single platform.

---

## Expected Outcome

MKpause is expected to provide a digital structure that supports both **students' autonomy** during moments of self-regulation and the **follow-up provided by the responsible professionals**.

The platform should allow relevant information to be recorded in an organized way and consulted later, according to the permissions set for each user profile.
