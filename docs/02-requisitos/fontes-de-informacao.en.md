# Information Sources

The definition of **MKpause** requirements draws on different sources of information to understand the context of the problem, identify user needs and guide the features proposed for the platform.

Using multiple sources makes it possible to analyze the problem from different perspectives and reduces reliance on a single source during requirements gathering.

---

## Bibliographic and Documentary Research

Bibliographic and documentary research is used to understand concepts related to:

- self-regulation;
- well-being in the school environment;
- adolescents' emotional health;
- student follow-up;
- accessibility;
- privacy and data protection.

This research mainly helps to understand the domain in which MKpause operates and to identify the precautions needed while developing the solution.

### Contribution to the project

Documentary research helps define:

- the context of the problem;
- the platform's boundaries;
- precautions regarding student information;
- the characteristics of self-regulation activities;
- non-functional requirements;
- accessibility and privacy criteria.

---

## Users and Stakeholders

The needs of the different users involved in the process are one of the main sources for defining features.

The main groups considered are:

- students;
- teachers;
- school psychologists;
- clinical psychologists;
- guardians;
- platform administrators.

### Contribution to the project

Analyzing these users' needs helps define:

- the features available to each profile;
- access permissions;
- forms of follow-up;
- the records required;
- alerts;
- notes;
- links between students and professionals;
- the information needed to use the platform.

---

## Elicitation with Potential Users

Contact with potential users and people connected to the school context can be used to understand the difficulties, needs and expectations related to the problem.

Applicable elicitation techniques include:

- interviews;
- questionnaires;
- observation;
- exploratory conversations;
- usage scenario analysis.

### Contribution to the project

These techniques can help identify:

- situations in which students need a pause;
- difficulties in the current process;
- resources students find useful;
- the needs of the professionals responsible for follow-up;
- information that matters during an alert;
- expectations about how easy the platform should be to use.

---

## Analysis of Existing Solutions

Analyzing products and platforms in the same domain shows how similar problems are handled by other solutions.

This analysis may consider:

- the features offered;
- how interfaces are organized;
- forms of interaction;
- strengths;
- limitations;
- user reviews.

### Contribution to the project

Analyzing existing solutions helps to:

- identify recurring features;
- find opportunities for improvement;
- define differentiators;
- identify usability problems;
- analyze interaction patterns;
- generate new ideas for MKpause.

---

## Academic Context

MKpause's development is also guided by the activities, content and modeling processes used in the **Software Engineering program at PUCPR**.

Academic activities help structure and document:

- requirements gathering;
- functional requirements;
- non-functional requirements;
- data requirements;
- business rules;
- use cases;
- conceptual modeling;
- logical modeling;
- prototyping;
- system documentation.

Feedback received during assessments and presentations is also used to review and correct the project's artifacts.

---

## Validation and Refinement

MKpause requirements are not treated as fixed.

During development, new information may require:

- adding requirements;
- changing existing requirements;
- removing features;
- changing business rules;
- revising actors;
- revising relationships;
- updating the data model.

When a change is made, the related documents must be reviewed to keep the project consistent.
