# System Purpose

This document explains why MKpause exists and why it takes its current form: a **structured pause** in the school environment, combined with **follow-up by professionals**.

The full description of the problem and scope is in the [Overview](../01-visao-geral/README.en.md). Here, the focus is on the rationale behind each part of the proposal.

---

## The proposal in one sentence

MKpause gives students a short, guided moment to recognize how they feel, complete a self-regulation activity and record how they feel afterwards, while keeping the responsible professionals informed when needed.

---

## Why a pause

Moments of emotional overload happen during the school routine, and there is not always a clear process for dealing with them ([Problem](../01-visao-geral/problema.en.md)). The pause creates that process: students step away from what they are doing, use a self-regulation resource and return.

The activities offered are grounded in research:

| Activity | Evidence | Document |
|---|---|---|
| Guided breathing | 54 of 72 breathing interventions reduced stress or anxiety (Bentley et al., 2023) | [Activities](./atividades-de-autorregulacao.en.md) |
| Casual games | Promising results for anxiety, stress and low mood (Pine et al., 2020) | [Activities](./atividades-de-autorregulacao.en.md) |

---

## Why record mood before and after

Recording the emotional state at the start and end of the session (RF04 and RF06) serves two purposes:

1. **For the student:** naming one's own state is the first step of self-regulation, and comparing before and after makes the effect of the pause visible.
2. **For follow-up:** the records build a history that lets professionals observe patterns over time, within the permissions of each profile.

---

## Why involve professionals

Bentley et al. (2023) identified **human guidance** as a core component of effective breathing practices. MKpause follows this principle: the platform supports students but does not leave them on their own.

For this reason, the system includes:

- links between each student and teachers, school psychologists and clinical psychologists;
- alerts for situations classified as higher need (levels 2 and 3, under RN12);
- notes recorded by school professionals.

---

## What MKpause is not

The platform is a **support** tool. Under business rules RN35 to RN37, MKpause:

- does not provide psychological or psychiatric diagnosis;
- does not replace follow-up by qualified professionals;
- is not an emergency service.

The research cited in this section supports design and content decisions. It does not make MKpause a clinical intervention.

## How the decisions connect

```text
Purpose: support self-regulation in the school environment
        │
        ├── Evidence-based activities
        │       ├── Slow guided breathing ........... Bentley et al. (2023)
        │       └── Casual games, no penalties ...... Pine et al. (2020)

        ├── A visual environment that adds no tension

        └── People involved in the process

```
