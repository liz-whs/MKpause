# Scientific Rationale

This section brings together the research behind MKpause's main design decisions: the purpose of the platform, the activities it offers, the color palette, the shapes used in the interface and the choice of mascot.

The goal is for every decision to be explainable and verifiable, rather than justified by aesthetic preference alone.

## Summary of decisions

| Decision | Rationale | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Offer slow-paced guided breathing | Most of the breathing interventions reviewed reduced stress or anxiety | Bentley et al. (2023) |
| Keep professionals linked to each student | Human guidance is a core component of effective practices | Bentley et al. (2023) |
| Offer simple, short games with no penalties | Casual games showed promising results for anxiety and stress | Pine et al. (2020) |
| Use light, low-saturation backgrounds | Brightness increases pleasure; saturation increases arousal | Valdez & Mehrabian (1994) |
| Use curved shapes and a mascot with a rounded silhouette | Curved objects are preferred; sharp contours may convey threat | Bar & Neta (2006) |

---

## How to read this rationale

- **Research guides decisions; it does not guarantee outcomes.** It points to the most likely direction. Final validation depends on testing with MKpause's audience.
- **Each document states its limitations.** Sample sizes, study quality and differences between the research setting and the platform are made explicit.
- **MKpause is not a clinical intervention.** The sources support design decisions, in line with business rules RN35 to RN37.
